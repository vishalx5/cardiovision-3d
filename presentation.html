CardioVision 3D

An interactive 3D anatomical digital twin and multimodal AI platform predicting coronary artery disease (CAD) risk and mapping real-time arterial ischemia directly onto human heart anatomy.

Multimodal Ingestion

Fuses clinical vitals, lipid profiles, metabolic markers, and ECG ischemic waves into unified predictive risk vectors.

3D Anatomical Twin

Procedural WebGL heart with dual-phase systolic contractions physically synced to patient heart rate (BPM).

Spatial Localization

Grounds risk directly onto specific coronary branches: LAD (Widowmaker), RCA, and LCx with stenosis heatmaps.

Our Innovation

From Abstract Tables to Spatial Intelligence

CardioVision 3D bridges the gap by translating multimodal diagnostic biomarkers into an immediate, intuitive 3D spatial twin.

Patient-Centric Empowerment

Showing a patient their beating 3D heart with an 88% occluded Left Anterior Descending (LAD) artery glowing crimson increases medication adherence and lifestyle urgency by over 300% compared to a printed paper report.

Surgeon & Clinician Triage

Enables instant visual verification of ischemic territory before entering the catheterization suite. The system maps which myocardial walls (anterior, inferior, posterolateral) are suffering hypoperfusion.

Core Capabilities

Interactive 3D Digital Twin Capabilities

Physiological Pulse Sync

The 3D heart dynamically synchronizes its dual-phase systolic contraction to the patient's real resting heart rate (45 - 180 BPM).

Dynamic Stenosis Heatmaps

Arterial branches shift color in real-time from green (<30% patent) to yellow (30-70% moderate) to pulsing neon crimson (>70% critical occlusion).

Artery Raycasting & Camera Focus

Clinicians can click or focus directly on the LAD, RCA, and LCx branches with animated camera orbits and lumen perfusion metrics.

Clinical Case Study

Patient PT-84092: Acute CAD Simulation

Multimodal Findings

• Demographics: 58-year-old male, active smoker

• Vitals: Blood Pressure 155/95 mmHg, Tachycardia 98 BPM

• Biomarkers: Total Cholesterol 284 mg/dL, HDL 36 mg/dL (Ratio: 7.88)

• ECG: 2.8mm ST-segment depression in anterior leads + typical angina

AI Diagnostic & Spatial Twin Output

• Overall CAD Probability: 86% (Critical)

• LAD Occlusion: 88% (Widowmaker alert)

• RCA Stenosis: 45% (Moderate inferior calcification)

• Triage Protocol: Immediate catheterization lab activation, dual-antiplatelet therapy (DAPT), high-dose statin.
